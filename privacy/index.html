Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Legacy Water USA provides educational information and in-home water-testing and treatment services. This notice explains what information we collect through this website and how we use it.

Information you provide

When you request a complimentary water test, we ask for a name, phone number, ZIP code and preferred visit day/time, along with details you choose to provide. We use that information to process your request, contact you, schedule service and maintain business records.

Service providers

Our website uses infrastructure and services, which may include Cloudflare for website hosting and lead storage, Resend for email notifications and authorized systems or service providers that help us follow up on inquiries. Access is limited to what is needed for those functions.

Optional Google Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to measure website page views, traffic sources and limited interactions so we can improve our content and understand campaign performance. The website does not load the Google Analytics tag until you choose Allow analytics. You can decline without losing access to any website content or request form.

We do not intentionally send your name, phone number or submitted form fields to Google Analytics. A generate_lead event may be recorded only after our server confirms a newly saved water-test request; this event does not include your contact information. Google Analytics may use cookies and collect device, browser and approximate location information under Google's terms.

Your choices

Select Privacy choices at the bottom of any page to allow or decline optional analytics or change your earlier decision. Changing your choice stops new optional measurement on this website; it does not retroactively remove data already processed. You can also use browser privacy settings to control cookies.

Contact

For questions about this notice, access or correction requests, contact info@legacywaterusa.com.

We may update this notice as the website or our service providers change.
