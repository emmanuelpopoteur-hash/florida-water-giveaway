What a visit can tell you

Ask what was actually tested.

An initial home visit can help identify the water characteristics that were measured and explain possible next steps. It does not, by itself, establish whether every contaminant is present or absent.

Questions about lead, PFAS, or microorganisms need tests selected for those substances, often through a qualified laboratory. We can discuss which information would be useful for your home.
