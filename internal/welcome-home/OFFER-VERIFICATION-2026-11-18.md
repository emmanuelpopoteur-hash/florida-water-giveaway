# Welcome Home — offer verification before first mailing

**Status: INTERNAL DRAFT · NO PRINT / NO PUBLIC PROMOTION**
**Pilot:** 5 curiosity letters (`sc01a`) + 5 comparison letters (`sc01b`), St. Cloud FL.
**Business owner:** Legacy Water USA. No production change or customer offer is approved by this document.

## Proposed message
- A: "Your home is new. How well do you know its water?"
- B: "Already received a water system quote? Before you decide, see another option."
- Shared purpose: offer the complimentary selected-indicator in-home water check AND an optional system/financing comparison; no purchase required for either inquiry.
- Do not imply Home Depot or another chain referred the homeowner.
- Tasting voucher is issued after a completed eligible water check and is not conditional on a purchase. It is not in the mailing envelope.

## Verify three promotional claims with **written source material**

| Advertised claim | What must be established before approving | Safer interim wording |
|---|---|---|
| "Systems starting at $3,990" | Exact Essential SKU, installed total vs base unit only, sales tax/permit/HOA or plumbing extras, regular cash price, service area, availability, eligibility, any required fees | "Ask about our entry-level system options" |
| "6 months no payments" | Exact lender + program ID; approval/credit criteria; whether interest accrues, is deferred, or is waived; APR/finance charges; first due date; early payoff obligations; any qualifying financed system and end date; written lender-compliant ad copy | "Ask about available financing options; subject to credit approval" |
| "RO included" / "FREE RO" | Exact RO model and installation scope; what qualifying whole-home package includes; regular independent price; whether RO is always part of an already-priced package; inventory; maintenance; whether other promotions stack; documented authority to advertise | "Ask about whole-home + drinking-water packages" |

**Specific RO warning:** If a package *already* includes RO as part of its usual advertised price, avoid suggesting that the homeowner newly receives a separately free item. FTC Guide 16 CFR 251 explains that "free" premium offers must be genuinely free relative to the regular price of the required product, and material conditions must be clear from the outset.

**Specific payment warning:** "6 months with no payments" does **not** establish "0% interest" or "no interest". The creditor's precise product (e.g., deferred-payment or deferred-interest) dictates the applicable Regulation Z disclosure obligations. Never conflate absence of a required monthly payment with absence of interest accrual. Obtain the lender-approved copy first.

**Pricing and treatment warning:** Do not imply the in-home screening determines that water is unsafe, or that a whole-home filter removes specific contaminants, without appropriate confirmed testing and substantiated model-specific performance claims.

## Pilot measurement discipline
- Group QR `sc01a` or `sc01b` identifies **the letter variant, not the individual household**.
- `__compare` suffix marks **button intent** in campaign attribution, not proof of an existing outside quote.
- Ask the caller which letter they received and whether they are **already evaluating systems**; do not collect competitors' confidential documents.
- Count mailed, inbound, comparison requests, confirmed appointments, completed visits, vouchers issued, signed sales and sale value.
- With only 5 per group, treat as an operational smoke test, *not* statistically conclusive creative testing.
