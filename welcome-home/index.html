Offer Details & Terms

Legacy Water USA offers residential water-treatment products. The free in-home visit is promotional and may include information about optional paid water systems. Our check reviews selected household water indicators; it is not a certified comprehensive laboratory analysis of contaminants.

Exact filling portions, eligible service area, pickup arrangements, food handling and storage requirements still need confirmation before the pilot starts. The voucher expires 30 calendar days after issuance following the completed visit. This webpage is a preview, not an active public offer.
