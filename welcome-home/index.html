Offer Details & Terms

Legacy Water USA offers residential water-treatment products. The free in-home visit is promotional and may include information about optional paid water systems. Our check reviews selected household water indicators; it is not a certified comprehensive laboratory analysis of contaminants.

One voucher per eligible participating household, handed out after a confirmed appointment and completed water evaluation. No water-system purchase or financing application is required. Voucher covers a traditional ¼-lb-size Dominican mini cake without filling and three separately packaged traditional fillings from La Casita Celebrations.

Exact flavors, portions, service area, bakery pickup arrangements, storage requirements and voucher expiration must be confirmed before the pilot starts. This webpage is a preview, not an active public offer.
