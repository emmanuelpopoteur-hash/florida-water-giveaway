Before you request

A few answers up front.

Is the in-home water test really free?

Yes. The initial in-home water test costs $0 and there is no obligation to buy a system.

What will I learn during the visit?

We explain the characteristics we actually measure in your home's water and discuss possible next steps. A home visit does not test for every contaminant; concerns such as lead, PFAS, or bacteria require appropriate specific testing.

Is my preferred day and time a confirmed appointment?

No. They help us understand your availability. After you submit your request, a specialist will contact you to confirm the visit.
